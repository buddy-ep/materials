1 Numbers & Place Value

2 Comparing Numbers

  • > greater than / bigger than / more than
  • < fewer than / smaller than / less than
  • = equal to
  • Always compare digits from left to right.
8,578 < 8,587  →  Same up to tens: 7 < 8 ✓
758,323 > 758,232  →  Hundreds: 3 > 2 ✓

3 Ordering Numbers

Ascending order = arranging from smallest to biggest

45,785   12,365   32,782   98,059
→ 12,365   32,782   45,785   98,059

Descending order = arranging from biggest to smallest

75,956   32,845   65,759   56,784
→ 75,956   65,759   56,784   32,845

4 Rounding Off — The Golden Rule

Look at the digit to the RIGHT of your target place:
✅ 5 or more → Round UP (add 1 to the target digit)
✅ Less than 5 → Round DOWN (keep the target digit)
Then change all digits to the RIGHT to 0.
