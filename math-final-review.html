Decimals

ทศนิยม

What is a decimal?

A fraction with a denominator of 10, 100 or 1,000, written with a decimal point.

Read the point as "and": 917.856 = nine hundred seventeen and eight hundred fifty-six thousandths.

Three kinds of decimals

  • Terminating (ทศนิยมรู้จบ): stops. ½ = 0.5
  • Repeating (ทศนิยมซ้ำ): same digit repeats. ⅓ = 0.333…
  • Non-terminating: keeps going. 2 ÷ 7 = 0.285714…

Place value & digit value

567.452
5006070.40.050.002
hundreds · tens · onestenthshundredthsthousandths

Expanded form

Add all the digit values.

97,284.624 = 90,000 + 7,000 + 200 + 80 + 4 + 0.6 + 0.02 + 0.004

Compare & order

Line up the decimal points. Compare digits from left to right. Add zeros to help: 26.5 = 26.50 > 26.35

Ascending = least → greatest (น้อยไปมาก)
Descending = greatest → least (มากไปน้อย)

Decimal → fraction

1 decimal place → ÷10, 2 places → ÷100, 3 places → ÷1,000. Then simplify.

2.35 = 2 35100 = 2 720

Fraction → decimal

Multiply to make the denominator 10, 100 or 1,000.

1725 = 17×425×4 = 68100 = 0.68   103125 = 8241,000 = 0.824

Rounding (การปัดเศษ)

Look at the next digit on the right. 5 or more → round up. 4 or less → keep.

45.908 → 46 · 45.9 · 45.91

Exam tip: Number of zeros in the denominator = number of decimal places. 562/100 = 5.62 (2 zeros → 2 places).

Ratio & Proportion

อัตราส่วนและสัดส่วน

Ratio (อัตราส่วน)

Compares two amounts. Three ways to write it:

3 : 1  ·  3 to 1  ·  31

Order matters! 6 : 2 is not the same as 2 : 6.

Equivalent ratios

Multiply or divide both numbers by the same number.

5 : 8 = (5×3) : (8×3) = 15 : 24
30 : 50 = (30÷10) : (50÷10) = 3 : 5

Simplest form

Divide both numbers by the biggest number that divides both (ห.ร.ม.).

45 : 55 → ÷5 → 9 : 11

Proportion (สัดส่วน)

Two ratios that are equal. Check with cross multiplication (คูณไขว้).

100 : 80 = 5 : 4 → 100×4 = 400, 80×5 = 400 ✓
2 : 8 and 6 : 27 → 2×27 = 54, 8×6 = 48 ✗

Finding the missing number

Write as fractions, cross multiply, then divide.

2 : 5 = 30 : Y
2 × Y = 5 × 30
2Y = 150 → Y = 75

Word problems: Keep the same order on both sides. If the first ratio is glasses : spoons, the second must also be glasses : spoons.

Triangles

รูปสามเหลี่ยม

By sides

  • Equilateral (ด้านเท่า): all 3 sides equal, all angles 60°
  • Isosceles (หน้าจั่ว): 2 sides equal, 2 base angles equal
  • Scalene (ด้านไม่เท่า): all sides and angles different

By angles

  • Acute-angled (มุมแหลม): all angles less than 90°
  • Right-angled (มุมฉาก): one angle is 90°
  • Obtuse-angled (มุมป้าน): one angle more than 90°

Angles in a triangle

The 3 interior angles always add up to 180°.

x = 180° − (45° + 35°) = 100°

Interior + exterior angle on a straight line = 180°.

Height (ความสูง)

A straight line from the vertex that meets the base at a right angle (perpendicular).

Perimeter (ความยาวรอบรูป)

P = side 1 + side 2 + side 3

23 m + 23 m + 15 m = 61 m

Area (พื้นที่)

A = ½ × base × height

½ × 14 cm × 19 cm = 133 cm²

Isosceles trick: vertex angle known → base angle = (180° − vertex) ÷ 2.  Base angle known → vertex = 180° − (base × 2).

Volume & Capacity

ปริมาตรและความจุ

Volume vs capacity

Volume (ปริมาตร): the amount of space something takes up.
Capacity (ความจุ): how much a container can hold.

Measuring tools

beaker · flask · measuring cylinder · dropper · syringe · measuring spoons · measuring cups

Units to remember

1 L = 1,000 mL
1 mL = 1 cm³
1 m³ = 1,000 L = 1,000,000 cm³

Converting

Big → small: multiply (×).
Small → big: divide (÷).

3.5 L = 3,500 mL
9,750 cm³ = 9.75 L

Mixed units

Split into groups of three digits from the right.

15,140,160 mL = 15 m³ 140 L 160 mL

+ − × ÷ with L and mL

Change everything to mL, calculate, then change back.

6 L 890 mL × 78 = 6,890 × 78
= 537,420 mL = 537 L 420 mL

Volume of a cuboid

V = L × W × H

30 cm × 15 cm × 40 cm = 18,000 cm³

Finding a missing side

L = V ÷ (W × H)
W = V ÷ (L × H)
H = V ÷ (L × W)

H = 288 ÷ (6 × 4) = 288 ÷ 24 = 12 cm

cm³ → litres: divide by 1,000. A tank 40 × 60 × 50 cm = 120,000 cm³ = 120 L.
