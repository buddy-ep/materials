Unit 3 · Lesson 1

Change of State of Matter

การเปลี่ยนสถานะของสสาร

Solids, liquids and gases are states of matter. Matter can change from one state to another when it is heated 🔥 or cooled ❄️.

A change of state is a physical change (การเปลี่ยนแปลงทางกายภาพ). The size, shape, volume, colour or state may change, but it is still the same matter. No new substance is formed. Usually it is reversible: it can go back to its first form by heating or cooling.

🔎 Explore the 6 changes

Tap an arrow or a button to see each change.

🔥 Heat up (add heat)❄️ Cool down (lose heat)

👆 Choose a change to see what it means.

💨 Two ways of vaporization

Evaporation การระเหย

A natural process. The liquid at the surface changes to gas.
👕 Wet clothes dry in the sun.

Boiling การเดือด

The liquid is heated until it reaches its boiling point.
🍜 Water bubbles in a pot (water boils at 100 °C).

⚖️ Effects of changes of state

👍 Advantages

  • The water cycle, which is important to all living things.
  • We use water in everyday life.

👎 Disadvantages

  • Big hailstones (ลูกเห็บ) can destroy houses and living things.
  • Climate change makes the polar ice caps melt → floods, and living things lose their habitats.
💡 Memory trick: 🔥 Heat moves matter to the right: solid → liquid → gas. ❄️ Cooling moves it to the left: gas → liquid → solid. Sublimation and deposition skip the liquid.

Unit 3 · Lesson 2

Dissolving of Substances in Water

การละลายของสารในน้ำ

When a substance is added to water and mixes well until it is uniform (the same all through = homogeneous), the substance dissolves in water (ละลาย). The solution has the properties of both the substance and the water.

Some substances dissolve in water. Others do not.

✅ Soluble ละลายน้ำได้

It can dissolve.

🧂 salt🍬 sugar🍶 vinegar🧺 detergent powder

🚫 Insoluble ไม่ละลายน้ำ

It cannot dissolve.

🛢️ oil🏖️ sand🍚 rice🖍️ chalk powder

🧪 Solute + Solvent = Solution

A solution (สารละลาย) is a mixture of a solute (ตัวละลาย — the thing that dissolves) and a solvent (ตัวทำละลาย — here, water). Solids, liquids and gases can dissolve in water.

🥤 Mixtures

A mixture (สารผสม) is two or more different substances mixed together.

Homogeneous mixture สารเนื้อเดียว

Homo = same. It looks the same all through.

☕ coffee and milk · 🧃 fruit juice · 🧂💧 salt and water · 🪙 coins (metals mixed together)

Compare: heterogeneous สารเนื้อผสม

Hetero = different. You can see the different parts.

🏖️💧 sand and water · 🛢️💧 oil and water

💡 Dissolving is a physical change. No new substance is made. Heat salt water until the water evaporates and you get the salt back — it is reversible.

Unit 3 · Lesson 3

Chemical Change in Substances

การเปลี่ยนแปลงทางเคมีของสาร

A chemical change (chemical reaction) changes the chemical composition of a substance. One or more substances change into one or more new substances.

It is irreversible: it cannot change back to its first form.

🤔 Physical or chemical? Tap to check

More chemical changes from the lesson: digestion, rotting, toasting a marshmallow, lighting a firecracker, baking a cake, grilling fish.

📋 Chemical change vs physical change

Chemical change 🧪Physical change 🧊
1. A new substance is formed.1. No new substance is formed.
2. Properties of the substance change.2. No change in properties.
3. Changes in inner structures.3. Changes in outer structures (shapes).
4. Irreversible or difficult to reverse.4. Reversible (can return to the original state).
5. e.g. fuel burning (combustion), grilling food, ripening fruit5. e.g. change of state of water, dissolving in water

⚖️ Good or a problem?

Chemical changes are important to living things and the environment, but some cause problems.

👍 Useful

🍳 Cooking · 💊 Medicine · 🌿 Photosynthesis · 🍽️ Digestion

👎 Problems

💥 Explosion · 🧨 Firecrackers can be dangerous

Unit 3 · Lesson 4

Reversible and Irreversible Changes

การเปลี่ยนแปลงที่ผันกลับได้และผันกลับไม่ได้

Changes in a substance can be natural 🌿 (ice melting in the sun, iron rusting in the rain) or man-made 🧑‍🍳 (cooking, burning paper).

↔️ Reversible change ผันกลับได้

A temporary change. The substance can change back to its original form.

Physical changes are reversible changes.

🧊 melting of ice · ♨️ boiling of water · 🍬 dissolving sugar in water

➡️ Irreversible change ผันกลับไม่ได้

A permanent change. The substance cannot change back to its original form.

Chemical changes are irreversible changes.

📄🔥 burning paper · 🔩 rusting of iron · 🍳 cooking

🔁 Can it go back? Tap to check

💡 Link it: Physical change = reversible = temporary. Chemical change = irreversible = permanent.

💡 Most of Earth’s water is saltwater. We can use only a small part (freshwater), so we must take care of it.

⛰️ Surface water and subsurface water

🌊 Surface water น้ำผิวดิน

  • Seas and oceans → saltwater (a lot of salt)
  • Rivers, lakes, canals and more → freshwater (little or no salt)

🪨 Subsurface water น้ำใต้ผิวดิน

  • Groundwater (น้ำบาดาล): water stored in rock spaces underground
  • Soil water (น้ำในดิน): water in the pore spaces in the soil

Unit 4 · Lesson 2

Natural Phenomena with Water

ปรากฏการณ์ทางธรรมชาติ: เมฆ หมอก น้ำค้าง น้ำค้างแข็ง

Weather (ลมฟ้าอากาศ) is the condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time. It includes clouds, rain, wind, temperature and humidity (ความชื้น).

Some weather phenomena are clouds, fog, dew and frost.

☁️ How do clouds form?

A cloud is a group of tiny water droplets or ice crystals floating in the sky. Tap ▶ Play or tap each step.

💡 Tiny particles in the air — dust, salt and pollen — help the droplets form clouds.

🌥️ Four main kinds of clouds

Cirrus

White, long and thin, like feathers. Made of ice crystals.

Cumulus

Big and puffy, like cotton balls. Round top, flat bottom.

Stratus

Flat sheets that cover the whole sky. Can bring rain or light drizzle.

Cumulonimbus

Tall and dark cumulus clouds. Bring heavy rain, thunder and lightning.

🌫️ Fog, dew and frost

🌫️ Fog หมอก

A cloud that forms near the ground. Water vapour condenses into tiny droplets around dust. It can make it hard to see roads, cars and buildings.

💧 Dew น้ำค้าง

Forms at night when water vapour condenses into droplets on cool surfaces like leaves, grass and spider webs. The temperature when this starts is the dew point (จุดน้ำค้าง).

❄️ Frost น้ำค้างแข็ง (แม่คะนิ้ง / เหมยขาบ)

Forms when the temperature near the ground is below the freezing point. Dew freezes into ice crystals. e.g. frost on Doi Inthanon in winter.

🧠 Knowledge vs Skills & Ability

📚 Knowledge ความรู้

What you know: words, facts and examples.

“Solid → gas is called ______.”

🧠 Skills & Ability ทักษะและการนำไปใช้

What you can do with what you know: look at a picture, a table, a graph or an experiment and think like a scientist.

“Look at the table. What happens when the water is hotter?”

💡 Skills questions often give you something new to look at. Read it carefully first, then use what you learned in Unit 3 and 4.
👀

Observing

การสังเกต

Use your senses to say what is really there: see, hear, touch, smell. Only facts, no guessing. Add numbers when you can (°C, mL, minutes).

⚠️ Never taste things in the science lab!

Sentence frameI see / hear / feel / smell ______.
💭

Inferring

การลงความเห็นจากข้อมูล

Use an observation plus what you know to explain why something happened.

Sentence frameI think ______ because ______.
🗂️

Classifying

การจำแนกประเภท

Put things into groups using one clear rule. Every item goes into only one group. Always say your rule.

Sentence frameGroup 1 has ______ because they ______. Group 2 has ______ because they ______.
🗣️

Explaining with reasons

การอธิบายและให้เหตุผล

Give a full answer with three parts: Claim (your answer), Evidence (what you saw or measured) and Reason (the science idea).

Sentence frame (C-E-R)Claim: ______. Evidence: ______. Reason: ______.
🔢

Using numbers

การใช้จำนวนและการวัด

Read scales and units carefully. Add, subtract or find a part. Always write the unit (°C, mL, km, litres).

Steps1. Find the numbers. 2. Choose + − × ÷. 3. Answer with the unit.
📊

Interpreting data

การตีความหมายข้อมูลและลงข้อสรุป

Read a table or graph. Find the highest, the lowest and the pattern. Then write a conclusion.

Sentence frameWhen ______ goes up, ______ goes up / goes down.
🔮

Predicting

การพยากรณ์

Use a pattern in the data or what you know to say what will happen next.

Sentence frameI predict that ______ because the pattern shows ______.
⚖️

Variables & fair tests

การกำหนดและควบคุมตัวแปร

In a fair test you change only one thing, measure one thing, and keep everything else the same.

Sentence frameI change ______. I measure ______. I keep ______ the same.

⚖️ The three variables ตัวแปร 3 ชนิด

Experiment: “If the water is hotter, then salt will dissolve faster.” This is a hypothesis (สมมติฐาน): an “If …, then …” guess we can test.

🔧 Independent variable

ตัวแปรต้น

The thing you change.

👉 water temperature (20, 40, 60 °C)

📏 Dependent variable

ตัวแปรตาม

The thing you measure (the result).

👉 time for the salt to dissolve

🔒 Controlled variables

ตัวแปรควบคุม

Things you keep the same.

👉 amount of water, amount of salt, glass size, stirring
