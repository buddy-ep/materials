📖 Talking about the future

BE + GOING TO + V1

Use it for:

① Plans you decided before (แผนที่ตัดสินใจไว้แล้ว)
I'm going to visit my grandma on Sunday.

② Predictions you can see (ทำนายจากสิ่งที่เห็นตรงหน้า)
Look at those dark clouds! It's going to rain.

SentencePatternExample
✅ ประโยคบอกเล่าS + am/is/are + going to + V1Pleng is going to sing at the concert.
❌ ประโยคปฏิเสธS + am/is/are + not + going to + V1We aren't going to play football.
❓ ประโยคคำถาม (Yes/No)Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V1?Are you going to give a concert in London?
❓ ประโยคคำถาม (Wh-)Wh- + am/is/are + S + going to + V1?What are you going to call your new album?

💡 Word order tip: Wh- → am/is/are → who → going to → verb → the rest

WILL + V1

Use it for:

① Quick decisions – you decide now (ตัดสินใจทันทีขณะพูด)
The phone is ringing. I'll answer it!

② Offers and promises (เสนอความช่วยเหลือ / สัญญา)
I'll send you the photos.

③ Opinions about the future – often with I think, maybe (คาดเดาตามความคิดเห็น)
I think the fans will love this song.

SentencePatternExample
✅ ประโยคบอกเล่าS + will ('ll) + V1Teacher Dan will help you.
❌ ประโยคปฏิเสธS + will not (won't) + V1I won't be late.
❓ ประโยคคำถามWill + S + V1?Will you come to my party?

🤔 going to or will?

SituationUseExample
I have tickets. It's my plan.going toI'm going to see the band on Friday.
I decide right now.will"I can't carry this guitar." – "I'll help you!"
I can see it will happen.going toLook! The singer is going to fall!
It's my opinion.willI think Plearn will win the contest.

⚠️ Watch out! Common mistakes

❌ Wrong✅ RightWhy?
I going to dance.I'm going to dance.Don't forget am/is/are (ลืม verb to be)
She is going to sings.She is going to sing.After going to → V1
He wills go.He will go.will never takes -s
I will to go.I will go.No to after will

💡 Exam tips

✅ plan / tickets / already decided → going to
✅ Look! / Listen! + you can see it → going to
✅ I think / maybe / offer / promise / decide now → will
✅ After going to and will → always V1
